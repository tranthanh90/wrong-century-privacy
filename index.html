Privacy Policy for Wrong Century Manager

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Wrong Century Manager is a private tool used only by the owner of the Wrong Century Facebook Page. It schedules and publishes the Page's own posts and reels, reads their statistics, and replies to comments on them. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can log in to it.

Information the tool handles

  • Content and statistics of the Wrong Century Page, such as posts, reels, views and reactions.
  • Comments that people leave on the Page's posts, including the commenter's public name and the text of the comment. These are read so the Page can reply.

How it is used

This information is used only to run the Wrong Century Page: publishing content, replying to comments and understanding which posts perform well.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent any information.
  • We do not use it for advertising or to build profiles of people.
  • We do not collect information from people's private profiles or private messages.

Sharing and service providers

All Page data comes from and stays subject to Meta's platform. The owner may use AI software to help draft posts and replies, so comment text can pass through that software for this purpose.

Keeping and deleting information

The tool keeps a short local log of what the Page has posted. It does not build a database of commenters. To ask for your comment or any information about you to be removed, send a message to the Wrong Century Page. You can also delete your own comments on Facebook at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a message to the Wrong Century Page.
